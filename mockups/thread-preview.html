Pull the thread

One sample hole, Tutankhamun to the Temple of Amada, built to try the feel before it goes into the app.

  1. Wait a moment on the stack: the card leans to show both ways. Swipe right (or tap the card, or press →) to go in.
  2. Move on: swipe up, scroll down, tap the passage, or hold it.
  3. Go back: swipe down or scroll up.
  4. Tap the blurred text to read the article. The knot at the bottom left brings you back.
Zigzag of the beads

Haptics: Android phones buzz; iPhones on iOS 18 give light taps; laptops show it in the thread.
